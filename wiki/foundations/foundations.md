# Foundations

Classical and precursor topics that the modern literature on coevolution,
open-ended search, and unsupervised environment design builds on. These
pages are background — they exist so that the concept and paper pages
can assume a shared vocabulary (selection, mutation, crossover, fitness,
neuroevolution, NEAT) without redefining it on every leaf.

## Pages

- [genetic-algorithms.md](genetic-algorithms.md) — the canonical GA
  template (population, fitness, selection, crossover, mutation,
  generations) that every page on this wiki implicitly modifies. Where
  modern descendants drop pieces (no fitness in MCC, no crossover in
  many neuroevolution methods), it is useful to know what they are
  dropping.
- [evolutionary-computation.md](evolutionary-computation.md) — the
  broader EC family beyond textbook GAs: evolution strategies (ES),
  genetic programming (GP), neuroevolution and NEAT, and the bridge
  from EC to modern deep RL via population-based training and
  adversarial environment generation.
