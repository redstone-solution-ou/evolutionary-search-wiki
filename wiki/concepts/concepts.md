# Concepts

Cross-cutting technical ideas that appear in multiple papers across
the wiki. Each page defines the idea, sketches the mechanics, lists
where it breaks, and points to the paper leaves that instantiate it.
Concept pages are the connective tissue of the wiki — they are how a
reader navigates from "I want to understand X" to "these are the
papers that put X to work, and here is what each does differently".

## Pages

- [automatic-curriculum.md](automatic-curriculum.md) — difficulty
  schedules generated by the algorithm rather than the engineer; the
  shared output of the coevolutionary and adversarial-RL papers.
- [coevolution.md](coevolution.md) — the central organizing idea: two
  (or more) populations whose fitness depends on each other. Splits
  classically into competitive vs cooperative; MCC and PAIRED both sit
  outside that dichotomy.
- [llm-driven-evolution.md](llm-driven-evolution.md) — keeping the
  EA selection-evaluation loop but replacing mutation and crossover
  with calls to a large language model that proposes code edits.
  AlphaEvolve and ShinkaEvolve are the canonical examples.
- [minimal-criterion.md](minimal-criterion.md) — a binary, non-ranked
  viability constraint as the entire selection signal; the engine of
  MCC and the methodological foil to fitness-based GAs.
- [novelty-and-quality-diversity.md](novelty-and-quality-diversity.md) —
  novelty search, NSLC, MAP-Elites; the line of work that established
  divergence as a usable search principle and that MCC explicitly
  contrasts itself with.
- [open-ended-evolution.md](open-ended-evolution.md) — the goal of an
  unbounded stream of interesting artifacts; why "no overarching
  objective" is a design choice rather than a bug.
- [parallel-and-distributed-ga.md](parallel-and-distributed-ga.md) —
  the island model: subpopulations evolving mostly independently with
  periodic migration. Originally a parallelism trick, now a structural
  primitive in modern LLM-driven program evolution as well.
- [regret-as-objective.md](regret-as-objective.md) — the
  minimax-regret decision rule that distinguishes PAIRED from
  domain randomization and from minimax adversarial training, with
  its link to classical decisions-under-ignorance theory.
- [unsupervised-environment-design.md](unsupervised-environment-design.md) —
  the modern deep-RL framing in which the environment-generator is
  itself the thing being optimized; formalized by PAIRED, with domain
  randomization and minimax training as the two prior corners.
